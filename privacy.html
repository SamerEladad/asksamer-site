Privacy Policy

سياسة الخصوصية

This privacy policy explains how Ask Samer collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use our website and services.

1. Data Controller

Ask Samer
Email: contact@asksamer.de

2. Data We Collect

We collect minimal personal data during pre-launch:

  • Contact information: Name and email address (if you contact us directly)
  • Website usage: IP address, browser type, pages visited (standard web server logs via GitHub Pages)

Booking: If you use the booking page, Calendly collects the information you provide (such as name, email, and chosen time) on its own platform. That data is subject to Calendly’s privacy policy.

Note: We do not currently process payment data on asksamer.de. Stripe or other payment providers may be added later; this policy will be updated when paid services launch.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data to:

  • Respond to inquiries sent via email
  • Organize and conduct booked calls
  • Improve our website and content

4. Third-Party Services

We currently use:

  • GitHub Pages: Website hosting (Privacy Policy)
  • Calendly: Tool for booking 1:1 calls (Privacy Policy)

When we launch payment processing, this section will be updated to include Stripe or any other payment providers.

5. Data Retention

Email correspondence and booking information are retained only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry, hold the call, or as required by law.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Object to processing or request data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

To exercise your rights, contact us at contact@asksamer.de.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

8. Home Address and Contact Details

We keep public-facing contact details limited to the email address above. No private home address is published on this website unless legally required.

9. Changes to This Policy

We will update this privacy policy when we launch paid services or add new data collection methods. The latest version will always be available on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at contact@asksamer.de.
