Privacy Policy

سياسة الخصوصية

This privacy policy explains how Ask Samer ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use our website and services.

1. Data Controller

Ask Samer
Email: contact@asksamer.de

2. Data We Collect

We collect the following personal data:

  • Contact information: Name, email address, phone number (when you book a call)
  • Booking details: Preferred time, educational background, questions submitted via Calendly
  • Payment data: Processed securely via Stripe (we do not store credit card information)
  • Website usage: IP address, browser type, pages visited (via standard web server logs)

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data to:

  • Provide consultation services and respond to inquiries
  • Process payments and send booking confirmations
  • Communicate with you about your appointment
  • Improve our website and services

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that process your data:

  • Calendly: Appointment scheduling (Privacy Policy)
  • Stripe: Payment processing (Privacy Policy)
  • GitHub Pages: Website hosting (Privacy Policy)

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law (e.g., tax records for 10 years in Germany).

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Object to processing or request data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

To exercise your rights, contact us at contact@asksamer.de.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at contact@asksamer.de.
